LOWLIGHT: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Lowlight is a game by LyfEscapes LLC (the developer). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it collects none.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, store, share or sell personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, collected memories and settings (sound, camera) are saved locally on your device. They never leave it, and uninstalling the app removes them.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock of the full game is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so the game can unlock; we never see your payment details. Google's own privacy policy applies to purchases.

Children

The app is rated for players aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address. Questions: support@lyfescapes.net
