Lowlight: Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026. Lowlight is made by t0ilet b0wl gaMez (LyfEscapes LLC).

What we collect

Nothing. Lowlight has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not receive any personal information from the app.

What stays on your device

Your progress, camera choice and the memories you have found are stored locally on your device so the game can continue where you left off. Removing the app deletes them.

Purchases

The optional full-game unlock is handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment under its own privacy policy. The app only learns whether the purchase exists on your Google account so it can unlock the chapters. We never see your payment details.

Permissions

The app uses the vibration motor for short feedback. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage.

Children

The app is rated for ages 13 and up and collects no data from anyone.
